What's New?

For anyone who's checked out the site before today, you might notice a lot of changes around the place. It's been a while since I've visited, and following some inspo from some amazing developers and gamemakers out there, it was more than time to update this.

So what exactly is new?

  • Font

The font has been changed to Silkscreen by Jason Kottke via Google Fonts. Fresh out of making games and messing about on Aseprite, I wanted to have a font that reflects that playful pixel energy. I'm still unsure on the readability of this, but for now it'll do.

  • Icons

With the font change, I wanted my icons to match. Gone is the modern Remix Icon library, instead replaced by Dazicons. Finding pixel icon libraries was surprisingly a lot harder than I thought. Also, a lot of them weren't in the 16-32-64-etc. pixel specification? Very weird. Thank you to Dazlog for actually having 16px icons.

Isocons still remains. It's a very cool, different looking library and I do love a good collage of things. It hasn't worn out its welcome on me just yet.

  • Contact Page (Removed)

Yes, the contact page is removed. It was a very small page anyways, and you will find that information at the bottom of the home page instead.

  • Other General UI

Just a lot of other smaller UI fixes, from font widths and colours and whatever else.
