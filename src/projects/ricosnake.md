# 2nd Place in the Ham Jam #2 Game Jam!

With the theme of 'Ricochet' and one week to work with, I and one other collaborator were able to create R.I.C.O Snake!
A snake-inspired action game, you pilot the titular R.I.C.O (Really Incredible Constrictor Organism) to avoid hazards and eat apples.

R.I.C.O Snake is probably my most polished game to date, and it is an incredible honour to have achieved my first podium ranking with it. Thank you to everyone who's played it and my wonderful collaborator paperplain7 for the music.
